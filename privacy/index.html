Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 September 2026

Ariana AI Assistant is a private, local-first creator assistance system developed for Lotiph_tv.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Ariana AI Assistant handles information when the account owner connects third-party services such as TikTok, Twitch, Instagram or YouTube.

2. Information Ariana may process

Depending on the permissions granted by the account owner and the connected platform, Ariana may process:

  • Basic account or profile information.
  • Creator-owned post, video or stream information.
  • Performance metrics such as views, reach, engagement, watch-time or other platform-provided analytics.
  • OAuth authorization information required to maintain an authorized connection.
  • Locally generated analytics, recommendations and reports.

3. Purpose of processing

Information is used only for the account owner's private creator workflow, including:

  • Social-media performance analysis.
  • Private weekly and monthly reports.
  • Content planning and recommendations.
  • Creator-owned performance comparison over time.
  • Approved local automation workflows.

4. Local-first processing

Ariana is designed as a local-first system. Core processing, private memory and creator analytics are intended to remain on the user's own computer whenever possible.

External services are contacted only when required for the connected platform's official API, authentication process or an explicitly requested platform function.

5. Authentication and authorization data

Ariana uses official platform authorization processes such as OAuth when connecting supported services.

Passwords should not be stored by Ariana. Where supported, access and refresh tokens are used instead.

Sensitive authorization data is not intentionally exposed in public status pages, public reports or this website.

6. Sharing of information

Ariana does not sell personal information.

Connected-account information is not provided to advertisers.

Information is transmitted to third-party platforms only when required to use that platform's official API or authorization service.

7. Publishing and advertising actions

Ariana does not independently publish content, start promotions or spend advertising money without explicit authorization from the account owner.

8. User control

The account owner controls which external platforms are connected.

Platform access can be revoked through the respective platform's account settings and by removing the local Ariana connection.

9. Data minimization

Ariana should request only the platform permissions required for the intended function and should not request unnecessary access.

10. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Ariana's platform integrations or data-processing functions materially change.
